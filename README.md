## What this is

1.1 is the first AWRAS airframe that actually got built. [1.0](https://github.com/morakah-hub/AWRAS-1.0) was the design, but the fuselage came out too small for the electronics, so it was made roomier and that revision became this aircraft.

It was finished and ready to fly in Qatar at the end of summer 2025. It never got off the ground — it came together too close to my flight back to the US, and flying it would have needed clearance I couldn't get in time. The airframe stayed in Qatar. The electronics came back with me and went into [1.2](https://github.com/morakah-hub/AWRAS-1.2).

Design details, airfoil study, and CG work are all in [1.0](https://github.com/morakah-hub/AWRAS-1.0) and apply to this aircraft too.

## The filament problem

The build stalled for most of the summer on one thing: I couldn't get LW-PLA to print.

**SainSmart LW-PLA did not work on my Ender 3.** It clogged constantly no matter what I tried with temperature, flow, and retraction, and the parts that did come out were poor quality. I lost weeks to it.

**Bambu Lab PLA Aero worked.** Once I switched, printing went from impossible to routine and the airframe came together in a few weeks.

If you're printing an airframe on an Ender 3, that's the single most useful thing in this repo.

## Build

**Wiring first, on the bench.** The full harness — F405, ELRS receiver, ESC, servos, FPV camera and VTX — was laid out and wired before anything went into the airframe. Easier to find mistakes on a flat surface than inside a fuselage.

  <img src="images/wiring-harness.jpeg" alt="Full avionics harness wired on the bench before installation" width="600"/>
</p>

**Assembly.** Superglue and epoxy for the joints, books and plates as clamps while things cured.
